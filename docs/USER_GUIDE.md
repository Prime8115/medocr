# MediScan — Quick Start for Pharmacy Staff

A one-page guide for the counter. No technical knowledge needed.

## Install
1. Open the download link your admin shared, on your Android phone.
2. Tap the downloaded file to install. If asked, allow "install from unknown
   sources".
3. Open **MediScan**.

## First time
- **Owner:** tap **Create one**, enter your pharmacy name, email, and a password.
- **Staff:** log in with the email and password your owner set up.

## Scan a document
1. On the **Scan** tab, either:
   - Point the camera at the prescription/bill and tap the big round button, **or**
   - Tap **Gallery** (a saved photo) or **PDF** (a file).
2. Choose the type — **Prescription**, **Invoice**, or **Auto-detect**.
3. Tap **Process**.

## Check and fix
- The app fills in the details it read. Fields with a **red/orange edge** were
  uncertain — **please read those carefully** and correct any mistakes by tapping
  and typing.
- Tap **Save** to keep your changes.

### Checking a purchase bill
- A bill opens as a **table**, the same way it is printed. Switch between
  **▤ Table** and **☰ Cards** at the top; the app remembers your choice.
- Swipe the table sideways to see batch, expiry, MRP and the rest. **Tap any row**
  to open it and edit.
- **Look at the bar along the bottom first.** It shows the item count and what
  the lines add up to:
  - **✓ green** — the lines match the total printed on your bill. You can approve
    without checking every line.
  - **⚠ orange** — they do not match. Something needs a look before you approve.
- The **Rate** column says where the number came from — `Rate (PTR)` means the
  bill had no separate rate column, so the PTR was used. MRP and Free qty have
  their own columns; scheme goods are never counted as billed quantity.
- Some suppliers print the same bill two or three times in one PDF (Original,
  Duplicate, Triplicate). The app reads it **once** and tells you in an orange
  message at the top when it has done so.

## Send it on
- **Approve** — marks the document as checked and correct.
- **Approve & Send** — checks it *and* sends it to your billing/inventory
  software (if your owner set that up in Settings).

## No internet?
- You can still scan. The document is **saved to a queue** (you'll see a number
  badge on the **History** tab).
- When the internet comes back, queued scans upload and process automatically.

## Find old documents
- The **History** tab lists everything. Use **Search** or the status filters
  (Needs review / Approved / Sent / Failed) to find a document, then tap to open.

## Tips
- Good light + a flat document = better reading.
- Always glance at the highlighted fields before approving — the app is a helper,
  not a replacement for your eyes.
